How the line is built

  • Each score adds a bump at its moment. A big score makes a tall bump.
  • The line is all the bumps added together, so medium scores that overlap stack into one tall hill.
  • Scores far apart stay separate hills. A positive and a negative at the same moment cancel.
  • Spread sets how many seconds a score reaches. Small is spiky, large is broad.

Things to try

  • Steady medium run: a run of +3 to +4.5 scores builds a peak far above any single score in it.
  • Few big scores: raise the spread and watch the hills join.
  • Dense burst: small scores packed into 10 seconds reach a big peak. Lower the spread to pull them apart.
  • Fade out: the line jumps at each score, then settles. This is the version that only needs past scores.
